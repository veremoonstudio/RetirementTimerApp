Privacy Policy

Retirement Timer · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Veremoon Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Retirement Timer Android application (package name: com.veremoonstudio.retirementtimer). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the app, and how you can contact us about your privacy.

1. Information You Provide and Local Storage

The app allows you to enter retirement target dates, work schedules (working days and hours), vacation hours, custom holidays, and background preferences. This information is stored strictly locally on your Android device using Android Jetpack DataStore. It remains on your device unless transferred via system-level Android Auto Backup services associated with your personal Google Drive account.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message contents, so that we can respond to your support request.

2. Device Data and Third-Party SDKs

The Application integrates specific third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) provided by Google to support advertising and in-app billing:

Google AdMob (Advertising): The free version of the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners process advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / AAID), device information, crash/diagnostic logs, and coarse location inferred from IP addresses to deliver, measure, and personalize ads. See Google's Advertising Technologies Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Information.

Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases): The app offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions (Lifetime, Yearly, Monthly Premium access). Purchases are processed securely directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial account details. Purchase tokens are processed locally to enable or restore premium features. See Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate the retirement countdown and save your preferences locally.
  • To serve advertisements in the free tier and process digital purchases.
  • To diagnose technical performance issues and prevent ad fraud.
  • To respond to user support inquiries sent to our contact email.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

App settings stored locally remain on your device until you change them, clear local app storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes local DataStore preferences, though system-level cloud backups may persist according to your Google Drive settings. Third-party SDKs (AdMob and Play Billing) process and retain data according to Google's retention policies.

5. Sharing of Information

Veremoon Studio does not sell personal information. Technical device data and transaction tokens are processed by third-party service providers (Google AdMob and Google Play) solely to provide app functionality and advertising as described in this policy.

6. Your Choices and User Rights

You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). You can remove locally stored app data through device settings (Settings > Apps > Retirement Timer > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app. For privacy questions, contact veremoonstudio@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Retirement Timer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information within our control. Data transmitted between the app's integrated Google SDKs and external servers is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

9. International Processing

Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing may be processed in countries outside your country of residence in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
