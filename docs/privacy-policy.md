# Privacy Policy

Veremoon Studio (hereinafter referred to as Veremoon Studio) respects the privacy rights of online visitors and users of Veremoon Studio products and services. Veremoon Studio recognizes the importance of protecting information obtained from users, and applies this privacy policy to inform users about the collection, storage and processing of information related to the use of Veremoon Studio products, services and the **Retirement Timer** application. This policy is valid as of the last revision. However, it is important to note that from time to time this policy may be amended to reflect changes and additions to the privacy policy. Before relying on privacy policy, check out the latest version of the document.

## Collection of Information
Users of Veremoon Studio products and services give their consent to the collection and processing of data related to their use of Veremoon Studio applications. By personal identification data is meant information provided by users during any exchange of information with Veremoon Studio regarding the use of the Retirement Timer application. Veremoon Studio does not transfer personal identification data to third parties, except as specified in this policy. In order to analyze consumer behavior, product use scenarios and demographic characteristics of individual users and groups, Veremoon Studio also processes anonymous data (including summary data). This anonymous data does not identify the users with whom they are associated. Veremoon Studio may transfer anonymous data (including summary data) to third parties.

## Personal Identification
“Personal Identity” is information that can be used as a unique identifier for a user’s identity, such as a name, address, or payment details. Personal identification information may be requested at the time of entry into the system, as well as when using products, services and Veremoon Studio applications. Veremoon Studio collects personal identification data on a voluntary basis, but for some Veremoon Studio products and services, personal data collection may be mandatory, for example, if it is necessary to gain access to a product or service, or to complete an order or in-app purchase.

## Use of Personal Identification Data
Personal identification data is used within Veremoon Studio for the delivery, development and improvement of products, content and services to which users have subscribed, as well as for responding to user requests. In addition, Veremoon Studio may allow third parties (such as Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases) to provide services under a contract with Veremoon Studio, such as processing orders and payments, accompanying orders, and serving advertisements, and also provide these parties with access to necessary data. The possibility of their use is restricted only to the extent necessary to provide the above services.

Veremoon Studio can use personal identification data of users to send information about Veremoon Studio, including news about product updates, contests, events and other marketing materials, but only if users have expressed a desire to receive such mailings. Veremoon Studio does not transfer personal identification data to third parties for marketing purposes without the consent of users.

External websites and companies that link to the applications and products of Veremoon Studio, which, in turn, are linked to websites and products of Veremoon Studio, may collect personal identification data of users. Veremoon Studio’s privacy policy does not apply to external websites, companies and third-party publishers. The collection and processing of personal data in such cases is governed by the privacy policies of the respective companies and websites.

Veremoon Studio may disclose personal identification information at the request of judicial or law enforcement authorities. In the event of a reorganization, sale, or merger, a company may transfer personal identification data to relevant third parties with the user's consent, if required by law.

The user can send a request to delete data or account at any time by writing an appeal to **veremoonstudio@gmail.com** or using the feedback form. The data will be deleted after confirmation of the request.

## Cookies and Data Stored on User Device
Veremoon Studio products, services and applications may use cookies, local storage (such as Android DataStore), and other technologies, such as web beacons and pixel tags. Local storage and cookies are electronic information units that can be transferred to a user's device to set up a product or application, store user settings (such as countdown dates and work schedules), and store information that a user regularly needs. We may also use third-party web and app analytics tools that use cookies or device identifiers to collect non-personal information related to the use of our applications.
